# Week 12 – Automobile Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing automobile data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard helps compare vehicles based on price, horsepower, engine size, fuel efficiency, body style, drive wheels, and manufacturer market segments.

## Objectives
- Create KPI cards to display important automobile statistics.
- Analyze the relationship between horsepower and vehicle price.
- Compare city MPG and highway MPG.
- Study the relationship between engine size and horsepower.
- Compare vehicle prices across body styles and drive-wheel categories.
- Analyze manufacturers across Budget, Mid-Range, and Luxury segments.
- Develop an interactive automobile analytics dashboard.

## Visualizations Created
1. Average Vehicle Price – KPI Card
2. Average Horsepower – KPI Card
3. Average Engine Capacity – KPI Card
4. Average Highway Fuel Efficiency – KPI Card
5. Horsepower vs Vehicle Price – Scatter Plot
6. City MPG vs Highway MPG – Column Chart
7. Engine Size vs Horsepower – Scatter Plot
8. Average Vehicle Price by Body Style and Drive Wheels – Matrix
9. Manufacturer Comparison by Market Segment – Column Chart
10. Final Automobile Analytics Dashboard
